## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Problem Statement

In today's digital landscape, sports enthusiasts deal with a disconnected environment when it comes to accessing quality training facilities, finding and booking reliable coaches for training relevant sports, and purchasing quality and appropriate sport equipment.

There is no centralized platform that connects all the key roles in the sport community like athletes, coaches, playground/sport complex owners, and sport equipment sellers. This results in poor communication, missed opportunities for collaboration, and inefficient service discovery.

Additionally, most local coaches and small scale playground owners often struggle with gaining recognition and managing bookings efficiently. Due to these reasons, many skilled coaches remain undiscovered by potential clients. Similarly, playground/sport complex owners usually rely on manual or outdated booking methods, which can lead to missed reservations, conflicts in scheduling, and operational inefficiencies.

The absence of an integrated system limits the growth of grassroots sports, reduces service accessibility, and hinders athletes' and enthusiasts' ability to develop by restricting their access to critical resources.

To summarize, the current sports ecosystem is highly fragmented, leading to several critical challenges:

- **Athletes and casual users** face difficulties in searching and accessing qualified and experienced coaches, and quality and reliable training/play venues. Without a centralized search system, this process is inefficient and time consuming.
- **Online sport equipment purchasing** lacks a trustworthy environment, forcing users to rely on platforms that offer unsatisfactory products and no verified community feedback.
- **Service providers** such as coaches, playground owners, and sport equipment sellers lack visibility and outreach, making it hard to connect with their target audience.

---

### 1.2 Solution Overview

To address the challenges faced by casual sports enthusiasts, athletes, and service providers, we propose a comprehensive multi-role, all-in-one, community-driven platform that connects every major stakeholder in the sport ecosystem.

This platform provides an intuitive web interface for users to seamlessly access and interact with sports services and resources in a single digital space. It effectively bridges the gap between casual sports enthusiasts, athletes, coaches, playground/sport complex owners, and equipment vendors — promoting collaboration, convenience, and community engagement through a unified digital solution.

#### Multi-role Integration

- Athletes seeking professional coaches and performance development.
- Casual users looking to book playgrounds for friendly games or recreational activities.
- Coaches offering personal or group training sessions in specific sports.
- Sport equipment vendors listing and selling sports gear through a built-in marketplace.
- Playground or sports complex owners managing facilities and renting out time slots.
- Admins overseeing platform operations, verifying profiles, updating news, and moderating user activity.

#### Core Services

- **E-commerce marketplace:** Browse and purchase high quality sports equipment with verified user reviews.
- **Coaches and playground/complex booking:** Search, discover, and reserve time with qualified coaches or available venues.
- **Community engagement:** Add ratings, write reviews, and interact through built-in feedback systems.
- **News and updates:** Stay informed with curated news feeds and announcements from the sports world.

#### Trusted and Transparent Architecture

- **Rating and review systems:** Allowing users to share their experience and assess service quality.
- **Moderation tools:** Enabling admins to manage user generated content and maintain platform standards.
- **Admin-verified profiles:** Ensuring authenticity and professionalism among coaches, service providers, and vendors.

#### Smart Features for User Convenience

- Role-specific dashboards tailored to each type of user.
- Booking management tools for handling schedules, cancellations, and availability.
- Real-time notifications for bookings, messages, and service updates.
- Search and filter functionalities for quickly finding services or products based on location, sport type, price, and availability.

## 2. Project Goals

The primary goal of this platform is to design and develop a user-friendly, scalable, and inclusive digital platform that connects every major role in the sports ecosystem — casual users, athletes, coaches, playground/sport complex owners, sport equipment vendors, and admins — into one integrated system. This also aims to enhance performance, participation, convenience, and collaboration in both professional and recreational sports activities.

This platform specifically aims to:

- **Reduce time and effort** required to discover and book sport services (hiring coaches or reserving playgrounds) by up to 50%, through intuitive search and filtering.
- **Increase income opportunities** for coaches, sports facility owners, and sport equipment sellers by enhancing their visibility on the platform and streamlining operations via booking, inventory, and customer management tools.
- **Simplify access to sports opportunities** for both casual users and competitive athletes, allowing even non-athletes to book playgrounds and organize friendly matches — promoting physical activity and wellness for all.
- **Support sports commerce** through an integrated online marketplace for browsing and purchasing sports equipment, complete with user reviews and managed by verified sellers.
- **Provide admins with comprehensive control panels** to oversee platform activity, moderate content, manage complaints, and ensure safety, fairness, and quality across all user interactions.
- **Empower service providers** including coaches, sports equipment vendors, and playground/sport complex owners with dedicated dashboards to efficiently handle bookings, manage availability and inventory, track orders, and communicate seamlessly with users.
- **Provide a trusted and transparent environment** through a verified review and rating system, empowering users to make informed decisions and encouraging service quality across the platform.
- **Serve as a central hub** for sports-related content, including news, ensuring users remain informed and inspired to stay engaged with sports.

## 4. Objectives of the Project

This project aims to develop a comprehensive online platform that connects sports players, coaches, ground owners, and shop owners, providing services such as bookings, equipment purchases, and coach scheduling — all under a secure and user-friendly system.

1. **Develop a Multi-Role Sports Management Platform** — Provide dedicated access and functionality for Users, Coaches, Sports Shop Owners, Ground/Complex Owners, and Administrators under a unified system.

2. **Enable Seamless User Registration and Engagement** — Allow general users to create and manage accounts, select sports, make payments, interact with coaches and shops, and book grounds easily.

3. **Facilitate E-Commerce for Sports Equipment** — Build an online marketplace for users to purchase, rate, review, and manage sports equipment from registered shops with secure payment options.

4. **Implement a Comprehensive Sports Complex Booking System** — Enable users to search for sports complexes, check availability, book grounds, request coaching sessions, and provide reviews.

5. **Support Ground and Complex Owners in Managing Facilities** — Allow owners to register grounds, manage availability, set pricing, define policies, and handle coaching and rental activities.

6. **Provide an Administrative Backend for System Governance** — Equip admins with full control over user verification, content management, booking supervision, shop and coach approval, and security enforcement.

7. **Enable Coaches to Manage Their Professional Services** — Allow coaches to register, manage schedules, accept bookings, track payments, and interact with users in a structured way.

8. **Integrate Communication and Feedback Channels** — Incorporate review, rating, reporting, and chatbox features for better interaction between users and service providers.

9. **Ensure Secure and Transparent Payment and Delivery Systems** — Support multiple payment methods with transaction history tracking and delivery detail management for both users and sellers.

10. **Establish a Scalable and Extensible Platform** — Design the system with the flexibility to add new sports, expand services, and adapt to future features like tournaments or live events (not in initial scope).

## 7. Project Constraints and Assumptions

### 7.1 Constraints

#### 7.1.1 Technical Constraints

- **Technology Stack:** System will be built as a web system using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL.
- **Browser Compatibility:** The website must work properly in modern web browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and be responsive for mobile devices.
- **Web Server Requirements:** System requires a web server with PHP support and MySQL database.

- **User Role Restrictions:** Each user can only register for one primary role but may have secondary associations (e.g., a user can also register as a coach).

- **Budget Limitations:** Development and deployment must be completed within the allocated project budget.
- **Timeline Constraints:** Project must be delivered within the agreed timeline.

### 8.3 Activity Diagrams

> *Note: The original document contains the following UML Activity Diagrams as images. Descriptions are provided below.*

**Registration Process**
User enters personal info → enters role → creates account → System prompts for email → User enters valid email → System verifies account → User selects role → Admin validates user info → System confirms registration → User logs in → System authenticates → System grants access.

**Profile Management Process**
User accesses system → Authentication system presents login form → User enters credentials → System checks validity → If invalid: retry or forgot password flow (initiate password reset → send reset link → complete reset → set new password) → If valid: User views profile dashboard → views/updates profile details → changes password (system verifies current password) → saves changes → views account history/transaction history → optionally removes profile (confirm deletion → delete account) → Logout.

**Playground Booking Process**
User selects sport → selects playground → views complex details → checks if logged in (if not: login/register) → checks playground availability → checks coach availability → checks sport equipment availability → selects a playground/complex → selects payment method (cash or card) → makes payment → Admin verifies payment → Complex owner updates availability → Booking confirmed.

**Coach Booking Process**
User selects sport → selects coach → views coach details and contact details → views reviews and ratings → checks if logged in (if not: login/register) → checks coach availability → views package details → requests coach booking → Coach accepts or declines request → If accepted: User selects payment method (cash or card) → makes payment → Admin verifies payment → Coach manages payments and updates availability → Coach booking confirmed.

**E-Commerce Shop Process**
User selects sport → selects sports shop → filters sport items → views item details → selects items → checks if logged in (if not: login) → adds to cart → views cart → optionally adds to wishlist → proceeds to buy → selects payment method (cash or card) → views delivery details → Admin verifies payment → Shop owner tracks order → offers discounts → responds to feedback.

**News Updates Process**
*(User side)*: User selects sport → views news.
*(Admin side)*: Admin logs in → selects news items → removes/updates/edits news → loops if more updates needed.

**Review and Rating Process**
User views rating → views review → filters reviews and ratings → checks if logged in (if not: login) → submits review → Organizational owner checks for issues → if issues: reports review + replies to review → Admin deletes review if necessary.
